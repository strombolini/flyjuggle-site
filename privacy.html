Fly Juggle Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

1. Overview

Fly Juggle ("the App", "we", "our") is committed to your privacy. This policy describes what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Videos and Media

Imported or recorded clips: When you import a video from Photos or record a clip using the camera, the App accesses that media only to analyze your juggling or to save a replay you explicitly request. On-device processing uses Apple's CoreML and Google MediaPipe frameworks; no video frames are transmitted for on-device analysis.

AI Coach analysis: If you use the AI Coach feature, selected videos and related structured analysis inputs (such as detected throw/catch coordinates and pose keypoints) may be transmitted to Fly Juggle server infrastructure to generate tracking results, scoring, replay output, and coaching feedback.

AI text features: Some features (trick recommendations, coaching advice) send short structured prompts through Fly Juggle server infrastructure to AI service providers in order to generate results.

Replay export: If you tap Save, generated replay videos are written to your Photo Library at your request.

3. Analytics

The App uses PostHog (posthog.com) for first-party product analytics. PostHog collects:

  • App events: which screens you visit, which features you use (e.g. "trick viewed", "AI coach started")
  • Performance and crash data to help us diagnose issues
  • A randomly generated anonymous device identifier used to distinguish sessions

PostHog analytics data is not used for advertising, is not linked to your real identity, and is not sold or shared with third parties for any purpose other than operating and improving the App. PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

4. Subscription and Purchase Data

If you subscribe to Fly Juggle Premium, the purchase transaction is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive a transaction receipt from Apple that we use to confirm your subscription status. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Analysis Data

Analysis results such as scores, throw observations, catch locations, timing metrics, and progression history are stored locally on your device so your past sessions appear inside the App. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use AI Coach, which requires server processing as described in Section 2.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • PostHog — analytics (US-based; anonymous usage data)
  • OpenAI / AI providers — short structured prompts for AI coaching features (no raw video)
  • Fly Juggle server infrastructure — AI Coach video analysis processing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertising networks.

7. Data Retention

Videos uploaded for AI Coach analysis are intended to be stored only as long as needed to deliver results. Analysis summaries and progression history remain on-device until you reset your profile or delete the App. Anonymous analytics data is retained by PostHog per their data retention policy.

8. Permissions

  • Camera: used when you record a juggling clip.
  • Microphone: used when recording a juggling clip with the camera so video capture works correctly.
  • Photo Library (read): used when you import a video for analysis.
  • Photo Library (add): used when you save a generated replay.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can revoke camera, microphone, and Photos permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Fly Juggle. You can reset all local profile and progression data from the App's Profile settings screen. To request deletion of any server-side data associated with your use of AI Coach features, contact us at support@flyjuggle.com.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after a change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: support@flyjuggle.com.
